# GovEase AI — Citizen Service Guide

A browser-based prototype that helps people explore common citizen services, prepare a stepwise checklist, find official portals, plan a visit, and open map directions.

## Prototype boundaries

- GovEase AI is an independent prototype, not a government website or service.
- Officer availability and queue estimates are sample values. No live government feed is connected.
- Visit plans are reminders only; they do not book appointments or issue tokens.
- Bribe SOS creates a draft note for the citizen to copy. It does not send, save, or submit a report.
- Applications, fees, eligibility decisions, and official requirements remain with the linked government portals.

## Features

- Search and filter ten common service pathways.
- Stepwise preparation checklists with progress.
- Direct links to official service portals.
- City greeting and map directions for sample service desks.
- Local My Desk bookmarks and visit reminders.
- English, Hindi, and Odia navigation, voice search where browser support is available, mobile layout, and printable checklists.
- Official reporting links including Odisha Vigilance, emergency response, and CPGRAMS.

This project is a demonstration. Verify current instructions directly with the responsible department before sharing personal information or travelling.
