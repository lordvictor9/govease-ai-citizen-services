Good to know

Before you apply

A few simple checks can help you avoid delays and scams.

Are these instructions official?

No. This is an independent prototype. The preparation lists are general starting points and may not match your case. Use the linked official portal for authoritative eligibility, document, fee and timeline information.

Can I apply or book an appointment here?

No. This guide does not send applications, issue tokens or make appointments. It can keep a visit reminder locally; use the linked government service to complete official actions.

How can I find a government scheme?

Open myScheme to explore central and state schemes. Check the current scheme page and the implementing department’s instructions before applying.

How do I report a public service grievance?

Use CPGRAMS for grievances covered by that system, or follow the official grievance channel for the relevant state or department. This prototype does not receive or forward complaints.
